# M2 plan: Spells and the stack

## Context

This plan covers M2, Spells and the stack (`docs/roadmap.md`). The project's goal is to handle
every tournament-legal card eventually, with no shortcuts (the scope test is in design.md §1). So the
plan does three things:

- it designs M2's systems (casting, timing, targeting, the stack, resolution, mana and costs)
  so that every rule and keyword that touches them fits later;
- it reviews the architecture against the hardest cases;
- it removes the rule defaults M0 and M1 hard-coded.

The user approved it on 2026-10-19 (approval notes below). Its first step, M2.0, saved it here
and made the documentation changes of section 9: the rules coverage map in `docs/roadmap.md`,
`docs/torture-tests.md`, CLAUDE.md rules 12 and 13, and design.md §17. The plan is deleted in
M2's last step (M2.11).

## 2. Architecture risk review

For each risk: what M2 builds, what later milestones fill in, and whether anything can't be
accommodated. Every item below is written up in design.md §17. Each subsection there is
provisional: the milestone that builds it may revise it, with the user's sign-off (decisions.md
records the exception to "same PR as the code").

1. **Reversing an illegal or abandoned action mid-casting (733).**
   - **M2 builds a checkpoint.** When an action begins, the task stores a `Checkpoint`: a copy
     of every restorable `GameState` field. That is every field except an explicit exclusion
     list: the log, the task stack, `pending`, the ID, timestamp and request counters, which
     only ever grow, and the RNG, which is never rewound, so a reversal can't be used to peek
     at randomness.
   - **Reversal:**
     1. Restore the checkpoint. The card is back in its zone with its old ID; tapped lands
        untap; pools and everything else are as before.
     2. (Superseded in M2.3: the kept abilities' recorded results are made again, never the
        abilities themselves; handoff notes.) Re-apply, in their original order and with their
        recorded choices, the mana abilities
        the player chose to keep (733.1). The kept set must contain every ability whose mana
        a kept ability spent; `check_answer` enforces that. Triggers from kept abilities
        trigger again; those of the undone action don't.
     3. Record knowledge gained during the attempt: the card was seen on the stack.
     4. Log a public `ActionReversed`.
   - **Library actions.** 733.1 forbids reversing actions that move cards to or from a library,
     shuffle, or reveal from a library. 601.2h pays such costs last, after payability is known,
     and mana abilities can't do them (605.1a). So a reversal after one is an engine bug: it
     raises `EngineError`, and a test checks this.
   - **"Abandoning"** is not a separate action. A player who doesn't activate mana abilities
     (118.3c: not mandatory) and can't pay from the pool makes the cast illegal, and it is
     reversed. A player able to pay must pay. Earlier stages offer only legal choices.
   - **Later:** M4 uses the same checkpoint for activated abilities and special actions, and
     its non-mana costs need nothing extra.
   - The alternative considered, recording an inverse for each change, couldn't restore
     exactly the objects M4's costs move.
2. **One permission and prohibition system where "can't" beats "can" (101.2).**
   - **M2 builds `resolve.engine.legality`.** Every legality question is an `Action` value:
     `CastAction`, `PlayLandAction`, `ActivateManaAbility`, `TargetAction`, `CounterAction`,
     `DestroyAction`. `allowed(state, action) -> Verdict(ok, reasons, rules)`:
     1. collects **permissions**: the rules' own (117.1a, 305.1, 601.3, 903.8) and those
        granted by effects;
     2. requires at least one;
     3. collects **prohibitions** (hexproof, shroud, protection, "can't be countered",
        indestructible as "can't be destroyed");
     4. any applicable prohibition wins (101.2). Abilities added or removed are not
        prohibitions (101.2a).

     601.3a-b's "consider later choices" is a flag on the check.
   - **Later:**
     - M3 adds attack and block actions;
     - M4 adds activations, special actions, searching, and event-level prohibitions ("can't
       gain life", "your life total can't change", "can't draw more than one card"), checked
       as events are proposed. Their order with replacement effects is settled in M6 (614);
     - M5 adds prohibitions and permissions from static abilities;
     - M9 adds split second.
   - Nothing found that can't fit.
3. **Fully general costs.** M2 builds the cost model (3.4):
   - mana symbols of every kind (107.4: colored, {C}, generic, {X}, hybrid, monocolored hybrid,
     Phyrexian, snow);
   - mana units carrying restriction and source data (106.6; snow-ness recorded at production,
     for {S});
   - a `TotalCost` of a mana part plus **cost components**, each with `can_pay`, the choices it
     needs, its payment events and its 601.2h ordering class;
   - the 601.2f pipeline of ordered modifiers: base (mana cost or alternative cost), plus
     additional costs, plus increases, minus reductions, in the order the player chooses where
     it matters (118.7e: hybrid halves; colored reductions), then "directly affects the total"
     effects, then lock-in;
   - **payment alternatives** (convoke, improvise, delve, waterbend, assist, harmonize) as
     extra options in the 601.2g decision, producing payment units only for that payment.

   M2 implements mana payment (generic, colored, {C}, X, restricted) and {T}-self. M4 fills in
   sacrifice, discard, exile, life, counters and tapping others. M9 adds hybrid, Phyrexian,
   snow and the payment alternatives. "Spend mana as though any type" (118.14) is a modifier
   of the payment check (M5). Nothing that can't fit.
4. **Stack objects that store every choice (707.10).** `CastChoices` holds everything 707.10
   copies:
   - the variant;
   - modes, with targets per instance of "target";
   - X;
   - divisions (M4);
   - alternative and additional costs chosen;
   - hybrid and Phyrexian announcements (M9);
   - the total cost and the mana spent.

   It is Immutable and lives on the stack object, so a copy is a card-less object with an equal
   record (M5). New targets (707.10c, 115.7) reuse `target_problem`. Abilities on the stack
   (M4) get the same record. The view's 111.1 gap for card-less objects is lifted in M5.
5. **"This object", and words as data (612).**
   - Definitions refer to themselves with `ThisObject()`, never a name (201.5).
   - Every color word, land type and creature or other subtype in a definition is a typed
     word: `ColorWord`, `LandTypeWord`, `SubtypeWord`. Card names are `NameRef` and never
     rewritable (612.2).
   - M5's text-changing effects (layer 3) apply a word mapping by walking a definition's
     Immutable fields generically.
   - M2 builds the word types and uses them in every M2 definition (Medallion's "green",
     Caller's and Lorekeeper's "Dinosaur", Repentant Blacksmith's "red"). It adds a generic
     `rewrite_words(value, mapping)` with a unit test, so the walk exists before it's needed.
6. **Values as expressions, evaluated at the right time (608.2h).**
   - Values are Immutable expressions: `Fixed`, `X`, `PowerOf`, `ToughnessOf`, `CountOf`,
     `GreatestPower`, arithmetic, and halving with rounding (107.1a).
   - They are evaluated through one `evaluate(state, value, context)`. The context says when:
     - on announcement (113.7a, 601.2b);
     - on resolution, once per effect (608.2h, 611.2d);
     - continuously, for static abilities (M5).
   - Object references resolve to current information if the object is where it's expected,
     else to its `LastKnown` (3.7).
   - 107.1b: negative results count as 0 where they set an amount.
   - M4 adds look-back (603.10, 608.2i) over a history derived from the event log ("dealt
     damage this turn", "spells cast this turn").
7. **Choices stored on permanents.**
   - M2 builds `GameObject.cast_record`: how the permanent's spell was cast, including kicked
     status and X (400.7d, 107.3m).
   - M6 adds `GameObject.choices` for "as this enters, choose" (614.12, 607.2a): color,
     creature type, player, card name.
   - M4 adds links for linked abilities (607: "exiled with this").
   - M5 settles which choices are copiable values (707.2, 707.9).
8. **Constrained answer spaces for every potentially huge decision.** A new design.md §6 rule:
   *a decision whose enumeration can grow combinatorially or without bound uses a constrained
   space.*
   - Existing spaces cover these:
     - X and life amounts: `ChooseNumber`;
     - targets, searches and proliferate: `ChooseSubset`;
     - division: `Distribute`;
     - orders of triggers and replacement effects: `ChooseOrder`.
   - New:
     - `ChooseCounts`, mana payment (M2);
     - `ChooseCardName`, any card name validated against the card data (M4);
     - `ChooseAssignment`, items each mapped to one of their choices with constraints, for
       attack and block declarations (M3).
   - Mode combinations stay enumerated: the worst real case is small.
9. **A general system for durations (611.2).** M5 builds it. A `Duration` value ends on events
   in the pipeline:
   - the cleanup step (514.2);
   - a turn beginning ("until your next turn");
   - an object leaving ("for as long as", "until ... leaves");
   - combat ending;
   - a condition becoming false ("for as long as", 611.2b).

   It is never checked ad hoc. M2 builds nothing, because no M2 card has a duration.
   Explore-style "this turn" modifiers of the rule defaults wait for it (M5).
10. **General counters and designations.**
    - **Counters:** `Player.counters` and `GameObject.counters`, `dict[CounterKind, int]`
      (122; poison, energy, experience, rad, ticket and the rest), arrive in M4.
    - **Designations** are a typed dict on players, objects and the game: monarch, initiative,
      city's blessing, speed, day/night, Ring-bearer, monstrous, renowned, saddled, suspected,
      goaded (with who goaded and a duration), prepared, Room halves unlocked, harnessed,
      solved, phased out. They arrive in M4 (object designations) and M7 (player and game
      designations).

    M2 builds nothing, and nothing in M2's design assumes their absence.
11. **Searching hidden zones, including failing to find (701.23).** M4 builds the `SEARCH`
    decision: the searcher sees the whole zone in the private request. It is a `ChooseSubset`:
    - minimum 0 when the search names a quality (701.23b);
    - exactly as many as possible for "a card" or "three cards" (701.23d);
    - nothing findable for an undefined quality (701.23c).

    Simultaneous searches decide in APNAP order (701.23i). Search modifiers ("only the top
    four", Aven Mindcensor, 701.23f) come from a rule-default function, `searchable_cards`.
    Knowledge of library positions follows design.md §9's "later" note. M2 builds nothing.

**Nothing on the list is impossible.** The heaviest are subgames (729, Shahrazad) and
restarting (727, Karn Liberated). A subgame is a nested `GameState` held by a task, whose
decisions surface as the outer game's pending decision. A restart builds a new initial state
from the cards. Both fit the pure-data state, and both are M9.

**(design.md)** marks what is written up, provisionally, in design.md §17.

### 3.1 Casting: variants, permissions and `CastSpell` (601.2) **(design.md §17.3)**

**Choices made before the card moves.** Every layout that changes how a spell is cast has its
player choose before the card goes on the stack, and is checked on the chosen characteristics
only:

| Layout | Rules |
|---|---|
| split | 709.3 |
| modal double-faced | 712.11b-c |
| transformed or converted | 712.11a, d |
| adventure | 715.3 |
| omen | 720.3 |
| prototype | 718.3 |
| face down | 708.4 |
| fused split | 702.102 |

601.3e generalises this. So the choice is part of what priority offers:

- **`CastVariant`** (Immutable), one of:
  - `NORMAL`;
  - `Half(i)`, `FUSED`;
  - `BACK`;
  - `Alternative(ADVENTURE | OMEN | PROTOTYPE)`;
  - `FaceDown(kind)`.
- **`CastPermission`** (Immutable): its kind and source, its zone, its timing (sorcery,
  instant, "as though it had flash", "during this resolution"), any alternative cost it
  requires (flashback, escape, "without paying its mana cost", 118.9b), and its leaving-stack
  destination.
- **`cast_options(state, player)`** (a rule default; 117.1a, 601.3) enumerates
  `CastSpellOption(card, variant, permission)`. Each must be `allowed` (3.11) on the variant's
  characteristics, and some legal choice of modes and targets must exist (700.2a, 601.2c). The
  cost isn't checked: an unpayable cast is reversed.

  M2 implements `NORMAL` from the hand, and the "during resolution" permission (M2.10). Other
  variants and zones raise their rule's `NotImplementedRule`: 709.3, 712.11b, 715.3, 720.3,
  718.3, 708.4, and 722.3 for a preparation card's alternative part; and 903.8 for a commander,
  until M7. **Corrected in M2.2 (user, 2026-10-21):** `characteristics` doesn't raise 109.3 for
  omen and preparation cards; outside an Omen spell on the stack they have only their normal
  characteristics (720.4, 722.4), so it reads their front part.
- **On the stack**, `characteristics` reads `obj.spell.variant` (709.3b, 712.8c, f, 715.3b,
  720.3b, 718.3b, 708.4).
- **On the battlefield**, the permanent keeps `cast_record` (400.7d): face up (712.13),
  prototyped (718.3b), face down (708), Room half unlocked (709.5d).

`CastSpell(player, card, variant, permission)` runs these stages and never gives priority.
Questions with only one possible answer aren't asked.

| Stage | Rule | Does | Asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PROPOSE` | 601.2a | Checkpoint (2.1). `SpellProposed`: the card goes to the top of the stack as a new object, with `spell = CastChoices(variant, permission, cast_from)`; a face-down variant moves face down. | — |
| `ALTERNATIVE` | 601.2b, 118.8, 118.9 | Alternative and additional costs. In M2 only those a permission requires (M2.10); a definition declaring others raises `NotImplementedRule("601.2b")`. | — in M2 |
| `MODES` | 601.2b, 700.2 | M2.7 | `CHOOSE_MODES`, `ChooseOne(ModesOption)` |
| `X` | 601.2b, 107.3a-b | when the mana cost is paid and the text doesn't define X | `CHOOSE_X`, `ChooseNumber(0, None)` |
| `HYBRID` | 601.2b, 118.13a | announce the nonhybrid equivalent; raises `NotImplementedRule` (M9) | — |
| `TARGETS` | 601.2c, 115 | M2.6, per `TargetSpec` per mode instance; then a public `BecameTargets` (ward, M4) | `CHOOSE_TARGETS`, `ChooseSubset` |
| `DIVIDE` | 601.2d | `NotImplementedRule` (M4) | — |
| `CHECK` | 601.2e, 601.6 | `allowed(CastAction)` again, ignoring the spell itself | — |
| `TOTAL` | 601.2f | the modifier pipeline (2.3), locked into `total_cost` | — in M2 |
| `PAY` | 601.2g-h | `PayCosts` (3.5) | 3.5 |
| `CAST` | 601.2i | public `SpellCast` | — |

### 3.2 Reversal (733) **(design.md §17.4)**

As 2.1: checkpoint, restore, re-apply kept mana abilities (superseded in M2.3: their results
stand as recorded, not re-applied; handoff notes), record knowledge, then
`ActionReversed`. The player keeps priority (733.2) and **the passes made before the attempt
still count**. Today `PriorityRound._acted` clears `passed` before the action; that is fixed.
`REVERSE_MANA_ABILITIES` is a `ChooseSubset` of the activations to reverse, with the dependency
rule checked by `check_answer`, and it isn't asked when nothing was activated. Nested actions
(a mana ability's own payment failing during a cast) use their own checkpoint.

### 3.3 Spells on the stack **(design.md §17.5)**

- `GameObject.spell: CastChoices | None`, set only on the stack. It holds `variant`,
  `permission`, `cast_from`, `modes` (each with its targets per `TargetSpec`), `x`, `costs`,
  `total_cost` and `mana_spent`.
- Nothing may assume a stack object has a card (copies, M5; abilities, M4).
- Face-down spells are visible only to their controller (708.5), through `knows_identity`.
- `ObjectView` gains `spell`, and `abilities` for permanents.

### 3.4 The cost model **(design.md §17.6)**

- `ManaCost` and `ManaSymbol` (107.4, every kind).
- `TotalCost(mana, components)`, where each component is a `CostComponent` with `can_pay`,
  `choices` and `payment`, and an ordering class for 601.2h.
- 601.2f modifiers: `CostModifier` values (increase, reduce, set-at-least, "directly affects
  the total"), gathered from static abilities that function where they are (113.6) and applied
  in the rule's order.
- M2 implements the mana part (minus hybrid, Phyrexian and snow), `TapSelfCost` as a component,
  and generic reductions. Section 2 item 3 lists what later milestones add.

### 3.5 Paying: `PayCosts` (601.2g-h, 605.3a) **(design.md §17.7)**

`PayCosts(payer, cost, paying_for)` pays the costs of spells, of mana abilities with a mana
cost, and later of effects (608.2g, 118.12). Its decisions go through `deciding_player`, and it
only uses the payer's own resources (723.5a).

1. **601.2g:** `ACTIVATE_MANA_ABILITIES`, a `ChooseOne` of "pay" and each mana ability the
   payer can activate (605.3a; 605.3c excludes the ability being paid for). This option list is
   where the payment alternatives plug in later.
2. **601.2h:** the pool is grouped into classes of identical units (type, source,
   restriction). `PAY_COSTS` is a **`ChooseCounts(options, maxima, total)`** answered with
   `Amounts`. `check_answer` checks colored and {C} symbols and 106.6 restrictions. Nothing is
   asked when exactly one answer pays; when none pays, the action is reversed. `ManaSpent`
   records what is left (118.3a, 106.4a). Non-mana components (M4) are paid here in 601.2h's
   order.

`Mana` gains `restriction` (106.6). Which units empty as a step ends is the rule default
`mana_that_empties`.

### 3.6 The auto-payer (agent side)

- `resolve.agents.autopay.plan_payment`: a deterministic search over the offered abilities,
  colors and classes. It prefers exact colors, keeps flexible and restricted mana for where
  it's required, then taps the fewest permanents. With no plan it answers "pay", and reverses
  everything.
- `AutoPayAgent(inner)` answers the payment, color and reversal decisions and delegates the
  rest. It is stateless.
- Used by `play_cli.py --autopay` and by half the fuzz seats.

### 3.7 Resolution, last known information and values **(design.md §17.9, §17.12)**

- `ResolveTopOfStack` (608.1); afterwards the active player gets priority (117.3b), with
  state-based actions first.
- **Instants and sorceries:**
  - 608.2b with `target_problem`: if every target, for every instance of "target", is illegal,
    the spell doesn't resolve and goes to the graveyard (`SpellDidNotResolve`); zero targets
    chosen isn't targeted (115.6);
  - 608.2c: printed order, then the chosen order of repeated modes;
  - 608.2h: values through `evaluate`, with last known information;
  - 608.2n: the destination function `resolved_spell_destination(state, spell)`. The default is
    the owner's graveyard; it is where adventure (715.3d), omen (720.3d), buyback (702.27) and,
    through M6's replacements, flashback-style exile plug in. The spell isn't moved if it
    already left (608.2m).
- **Permanent spells:** 608.3a; a permanent spell with a target (Auras, bestow, mutate) raises
  `NotImplementedRule("608.3b")`; 608.3e is checked. `cast_record` is kept.
- **Counter:** `SpellCountered` (701.6) uses the same destination function. It does nothing to
  a spell that can't be countered: `allowed(CounterAction)` is a prohibition (113.6g).
- **Last known information:** `move_object` records `LastKnown` (characteristics, controller,
  owner, zone, status, damage) for an object leaving the battlefield or the stack, in
  `GameState.last_known`. Entries are cleared when a player is about to receive priority with
  an empty stack.
- `ResolveSpell` walks the effects with a cursor, so effects that ask questions pause and
  resume.

### 3.8 Definitions, effects and targets **(design.md §17.10)**

- `CardDefinition.spell: SpellAbility(modes=(Mode(targets, effects, cost=None), ...),
  choose=ModeChoice(min, max, repeatable))` (113.3a). `Mode.cost` and `ModeChoice` leave room

- **References:** `TargetRef`, `ThisObject`, `You`, `ControllerOf`.

- **Values** as in 2.6.
- **Filters and groups:** `ObjectFilter`, `EachCreature(except_)`, `EachOpponent`,
  `SpellFilter`.
- **Targets:** `TargetSpec(objects, players, spells, minimum, maximum)`: 115.2, 115.4, 115.5,
  115.3; room for overload's "each" and for conditional targets (601.2c).
- **Effects:**
  - `DealDamage`: one 120.3 results table reading the source's (last known) characteristics.
    Deathtouch, lifelink, infect, wither and toxic plug in there; 702.16e raises
    `NotImplementedRule` there until M6. 120.8: 0 damage isn't dealt.
  - `Destroy` (701.8a, through `allowed(DestroyAction)`);
  - `Exile` (701.13a);
  - `GainLife` (119.3, 119.10);
  - `DrawCards`;

### 3.10 Keywords and targeting **(design.md §17.11)**

**Keywords and qualities.**

- Keyword abilities are `Ability` subclasses in `resolve.cards.keywords`: `Flash`,
  `Hexproof(quality=None)`, `Shroud`, `Indestructible`, `Protection(quality)`.
- Qualities are Immutable predicates over typed words:
  - `ColorQuality`, `MulticoloredQuality`, `MonocoloredQuality`;
  - `CardTypeQuality` (702.16a);
  - `SubtypeQuality`, `Everything` (702.16j);
  - room for `PlayerQuality` (702.16k).
- "Each" and "from A and from B" expand into separate abilities (702.16g-h, 702.11f-g).
- They are read only through `characteristics(...).abilities` (`has_keyword`), so M5's grants
  need no change.
- Players' abilities come from the rule default `player_abilities` (empty until M5).

**`target_problem(state, targeter, spec, candidate)`**, built on `allowed(TargetAction)`:

1. kind and zone (115.2, 115.4; a player still in the game, 800.4a);
2. the spec's filter;
3. not itself (115.5);
4. prohibitions from the candidate's abilities:
   - shroud (702.18a);
   - hexproof: opponents of the candidate's controller, or of the player (702.11b-c);
   - hexproof from a quality (702.11d);
   - protection: spells with the quality, or abilities from sources with it, whoever controls
     them (702.16b, j).

`Targeter` is the controller, spell-or-ability, and characteristics: the spell's own, or the
source's last known (113.7a). The prohibition predicate is a pure function, so player hexproof
is unit-tested now. It is used at 601.2c, at 608.2b, by 115.7 (M5), and by abilities (M4).

**Indestructible** is `allowed(DestroyAction)` refusing (702.12b), used by `Destroy` and
704.5g. 704.5f, exile and sacrifice are unaffected.

**Flash** gives instant timing in `may_cast_now`, from any zone the card could be cast from
(702.8a).

### 3.11 Timing, legality and visibility

- `may_cast_now` and `has_sorcery_timing` (117.1a, 307.1, 304.1, 702.8) are rule defaults;
  permissions may change timing.
- `allowed` (2.2) is the single legality check.
- `knows_identity(state, obj, viewer)` replaces `is_known_to` (400.2, 402.3, 708.5, 723.4).

## 8. The fuzz harness once spells exist

- **Decks:**
  - `tools/excerpts/cards.json` holds every registered non-basic card; a test fails if one is
    missing.
  - Two recipes are cycled among the seats:
    - **spells:** basics plus every noncreature card. Long games.
    - **creatures:** basics plus every card except the exclusion list. These end at the 508.1
      gap soon after a creature can attack.
  - Every other commander-format game gives the seats commanders (vanilla creatures), so
    Command Tower and Arcane Signet make mana; such games may stop at 903.8.
- **Seats:** odd seats are `AutoPayAgent(RandomAgent)`, from M2.5.
- **Allowed gaps:** 508.1 and 903.8.
- **Summary lines:**
  - spells cast, resolved, didn't resolve, countered and reversed;
  - casts per card;
  - damage dealt;
  - endings by life total;
  - gaps by rule.
- **Quick run:** 10 games, at most about a minute (measured in M2.3). `test_fuzz_summary.py`
  checks that it casts, resolves, counters and reverses spells, and that its decks hold the
  excerpt minus the exclusion list.
- **`--thorough`:** 100 games with every check. Its expectations go into
  `docs/verification.md` in M2.11:
  - every card cast at least once;
  - resolved, didn't resolve, countered and reversed all above zero;
  - endings by decking, concession and life total;
  - the only gaps are 508.1 and 903.8;
  - no `FAILED:`.
